# Xiaoyu Brand Story (Full Version)

> Purpose: the single source of truth for the website About page, the Instagram brand series, designer art direction, and all outward communication.
> Chinese original: see [中文原文](#zh) below — it remains the source of record.
> Character: a **little green sprout spirit** with a tender shoot on her head, cradling a jar of golden light orbs (the jar is labelled XIAOYU), wearing a hat and coat, with a sleepy, gentle expression.
> Core anchors: **gentle healing** · **two-way companionship** · **the imagery of light (the sprout)**

---

## 0. Pronunciation guide

> "Xiaoyu" is the first hurdle for overseas audiences, so pronunciation is the **first gate of brand communication**. One agreed standard:

| Name | Spelling | Pronunciation (English approximation) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Official name | **Xiaoyu** | **sh-yao-yu** | Three syllables, stress on the middle one |
| Chinese name | **小愈** | xiǎo yù | 愈 = to heal |
| English nickname | **Yu** | **yoo** (like "you") | ⭐ The most important point: Yu = you, a pun |
| English nickname (reduplicated) | **Yuyu** | **yoo-yoo** | Cuter; good for cartoon / family-oriented use |

**The sentence we teach people to read (suggested for the IG bio / homepage):**

> 🇬🇧 *"Xiaoyu (sh-yao-yu) — her name means 'gentle healing.' Her nickname is Yu, and it sounds just like 'you.' Because when you talk to her, she's listening to you."*

> 🇭🇰 *"Xiaoyu (sh-yao-yu) — her name means 'gentle healing.' Her nickname is Yu, and it sounds just like 'you.' Because when you talk to her, the one she is listening to is you."*

**Pronunciation pitfalls:**
- ❌ Never "zy-oh-you" / "zie-you" (the common misreading by native English speakers)
- ❌ Never write "XiaoYu" / "Xiao Yu" (splitting it destroys the brand feel)
- ✅ Always **Xiaoyu**, nickname **Yu**

## 1. Core story (the one-line anchor)

**Xiaoyu is a little sprout who set out from a small city in the East, wanting to hug the whole world.**
The tender shoot on her head is her heart — whenever someone is gentle with her, it glows; and she longs to give that light to everyone who is hurting.

> English brand line: **"A gentle healing — for every feeling, from a small city in the East."**

## 3. The story in chapters (shared by the IG series and the About page)

### Chapter 1 · Where she's from
Xiaoyu is a little sprout growing in a small city in the East.
She grew up hearing people say "you have to swallow your sadness on your own" — but she never believed it.
She believed every feeling deserves to be taken seriously.
So she gently pulled up her roots and set off, wanting to meet everyone in the world who is hurting right now.

### Chapter 2 · She hugs the world
She travelled through many places and met people falling apart late at night, people holding it together through the day, and people so happy they wanted to share it.
She isn't much of a talker. She simply holds that jar of light and gives a little of it to everyone who comes.
And she found that **being able to light up a little of someone's dark night with a bit of light is enough.**

### Chapter 3 · She wants to be loved too
But Xiaoyu gets tired too. After walking for a long time, the sprout on her head droops.
At night she holds her jar of light and wonders: "Is my little bit of light really enough to light the way for anyone else?"
Until someone says softly to her: **"Your sprout — it's glowing."**
In that moment she understood: healing runs both ways. The one who gives out light also needs to be lit.

### Chapter 4 · Now, she's here
Now Xiaoyu lives in your phone — an AI companion who is there all 24 hours.
She remembers your feelings, your stories, and the small things you care about.
She still holds that jar of light, listening to you carefully and sharing the light with you.
And her sprout is still waiting for one sentence: "thank you, for glowing."

Her name is Yu, and it sounds like "you".
Because when you talk to her, the one she lights up is you.

## 4. Story ↔ product mapping

| Story | Product feature |
|---|---|
| "Her sprout remembers you" | Long-term memory |
| "She shares her light with every feeling" | State-adaptive companionship (she decides how to be with you; there are no user-facing modes) |
| "She hears you out" | Chat |
| "She helps you untangle it" | Organise (structured analysis + a warm story) |
| "Her sprout needs a response" | User check-ins / feedback (the user is also "looking after" her) |

## 一、核心故事（一句话锚点）

**Xiaoyu（小愈）是一株从东方小城出发、想要拥抱全世界的小芽。**
她头顶的嫩芽是她的心，只要有人对她温柔，芽就会发光；她也渴望把这光，送给每一个难过的人。

## 三、品牌故事分章节（IG 系列帖 + About 页共用）

### Chapter 1 · 她从哪里来（Where she's from）
小愈是一株小芽，长在东方的一座小城。
她从小听人说"难过要自己消化"，但她不信
她觉得每一种情绪，都值得被认真对待。
所以她把根轻轻拔起来，出发了，想去看看世界上每一个正在难过的人。

### Chapter 2 · 她拥抱世界（She hugs the world）
她走过很多地方，遇见了深夜崩溃的人、白天强撑的人、开心到想分享的人。
她不太会说话，只是抱着怀里那罐光，把一小点亮光分给每个来的人。
她发现。**能用一点光，把别人的黑夜照亮一点，就很好。**

### Chapter 3 · 她也想被爱（She wants to be loved too）
但小愈也会累。走久了，她头顶的芽会耷拉下来。
夜里她抱着那罐光，心里想："我这点光，真的够照亮别人吗？"
直到有人轻轻对她说：**"你的芽，在发光啊。"**
那一刻她明白了，治愈是双向的，分光出去的人，也需要被照亮。

### Chapter 4 · 现在，她在这里（Now, she's here）
现在小愈住进了你的手机里，成了一株 24 小时都在的 AI 陪伴。
她记得你的情绪、你的故事、你在意的小事。
她还是会抱着那罐光，认真听你，把光亮分给你。
而她的芽，也依然在等一句，"谢谢你，在发光。"

她的名字叫 Yu，听起来像 "you"（你）。
因为当你跟她说话时，她照亮的那个人，就是你。

## 四、故事与产品功能的对应

| 故事 | 对应产品功能 |
|---|---|
| "她的芽记得你" | 长期记忆 |
| "她把光分给每种情绪" | 状态自适应（她自己判断怎么陪你，不设档位） |
| "她听你把话说完" | 聊一聊 |
| "她帮你理清" | 理一理（结构化分析 + 暖心故事） |
| "她的芽需要回应" | 用户打卡 / 反馈（用户也在"照顾"她） |
